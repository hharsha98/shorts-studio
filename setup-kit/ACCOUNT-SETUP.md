# Account setup kit: The Inner Tick (EN) / So tickt's (DE)

About 45 minutes for all 6 accounts. Do it on your phone (TikTok, Instagram) and your Mac (YouTube).

**Why you do this part yourself:** the platforms ban accounts made by bots. Payouts later need ID checks in your
real name. And an AI agent that holds your passwords (Claude, Hermes or any other) can be tricked by a web page
into misusing them. So please never paste a password into any chat. Nothing in this kit needs one.

## 2. Before you start (15 min)

- [ ] **Password manager.** On your Mac, Apple's built-in **Passwords** app is free, syncs to your iPhone, and can also
      create 2FA codes. (Alternative: Bitwarden, free and works on every system.) Let it **generate a new random
      password for every account**. Never reuse one.
- [ ] **One new Gmail address for the brand**, for example `theinnertick.studio@gmail.com` (pick any free name).
      This Google account will own **both** YouTube channels.
- [ ] **One inbox, many addresses (Gmail "plus addresses").** Everything after a `+` still lands in the same inbox. So you can
      give each account its own address:
      `…+tiktok.en@gmail.com`, `…+tiktok.de@gmail.com`, `…+ig.en@gmail.com`, `…+ig.de@gmail.com`.
      If a platform refuses the `+`, use your phone number for that one instead.
- [ ] **2FA (two-step login)** on every account: after the password, the app also asks for a 6-digit code.
      Save each account's **backup codes** in the password manager. Without them you can lose the account if your phone breaks.

## 7. Impressum (legal notice)

*General information, not legal advice. Confirm with the IHK start-up advice (Gründungsberatung) before you publish.*

In Germany, online services that are run as a business usually need an **Impressum**: a page that says who is behind
the account. The rule is § 5 DDG. A channel you plan to earn money with most likely counts. **One Impressum page can
cover all 6 accounts.**

### Draft text (fill in the [brackets])
```
Impressum

Angaben gemäß § 5 DDG

[Vorname Nachname]
[Straße Hausnummer]
[PLZ Ort]
Deutschland

Kontakt
E-Mail: [Marken-E-Mail]
[Zweiter schneller Kontaktweg, z. B. Telefon oder Kontaktformular, falls die IHK es empfiehlt]

[Nur falls vorhanden: Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer gemäß § 27a UStG: DE…]
[Nur falls vorhanden: Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer gemäß § 139c AO: …]

Verantwortlich für den Inhalt nach § 18 Abs. 2 MStV:
[Vorname Nachname, Anschrift wie oben]

Dieses Impressum gilt für:
YouTube, TikTok und Instagram: @theinnertick (The Inner Tick) und @sotickts (So tickt's)
```

### Where to put it
- **YouTube:** the "Links" field in the channel's "Basic info" settings (section 3, step 4).
- **Instagram:** the external link in your bio.
- **TikTok:** the "Website" field, if your account has one. If not, see question 2 below.
- **The page itself:** a simple free page is enough, for example **Google Sites** under the brand Google account.
  Ask the IHK whether that page also needs a privacy notice (Datenschutzerklärung).

### Questions to add to your IHK appointment
1. Which address may I use? The Impressum needs an address where letters and court papers can reach me (a
   "ladungsfähige Anschrift"), so a P.O. box is not allowed. Is my German home address the only option?
   Are paid "Impressum address" services acceptable? Is my address in India acceptable?
   (Don't pay for any service before you've told me the name and price.)
2. TikTok may not offer a link field to new accounts. How do I make the Impressum "easily reachable" there?
3. Does my explainer content count as "journalistic-editorial"? If so, the § 18 MStV line is needed.
4. Do I need the Impressum from the very first post, before I earn anything?

⚠️ **Privacy trade-off:** whatever address you put in the Impressum becomes public and is linked to your real name.
That's the main reason to ask about the address options first.
